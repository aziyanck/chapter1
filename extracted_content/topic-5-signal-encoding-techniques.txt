Definition of Signal Encoding & the Four Encoding Techniques

Key Concepts:
  - Signal encoding: Converting data into a signal suitable for transmission over a medium
  - Digital data → Digital signal
  - Digital data → Analog signal
  - Analog data → Digital signal
  - Analog data → Analog signal

--- 1. Signal Encoding Techniques  (Definition & the Four Techniques) ---
Definition: Signal encoding is the process of converting data (digital or analog) into a signal (digital or analog) that is suitable for transmission over a communication medium.
The Four Encoding Techniques:
  - Digital Data → Digital Signal — Example: A computer sending data to another computer over an Ethernet (LAN) cable.
  - Digital Data → Analog Signal — Example: A Wi-Fi router or dial-up modem sending digital data as radio or telephone-line waves.
  - Analog Data → Digital Signal — Example: Your voice on a mobile call being digitized, or a song recorded as an MP3 file.
  - Analog Data → Analog Signal — Example: AM and FM radio broadcasting a presenter's voice.

Exam Tips:
  - Define signal encoding.
  - List the four signal encoding techniques with a one-line definition of each.
